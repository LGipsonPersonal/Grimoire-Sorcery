Grimoire — Help

A quick guide to building decks

This page explains how to use Grimoire without needing any technical knowledge. If something isn't covered here, most buttons in the app also have a small pop-up tip if you hover your mouse over them for a moment.

The basics

Grimoire is a program for building card decks for the game Sorcery: Contested Realm. It works without an internet connection — everything you need is already on your computer. You only need to go online if you want to check for newly released cards.

The screen is split into three parts:

  • Left side — Find cards. Search and filter the full list of cards.
  • Middle — Card library. Every card that matches your search, shown as tiles.
  • Right side — Your deck. The deck you're currently building.

Finding cards

Use the boxes and buttons on the left to narrow down the card list:

  • Name — type part of a card's name.
  • Rules text — search for a word or phrase in what a card does.
  • Type — click Avatar, Site, Minion, Magic, Artifact, or Aura to show only that kind of card. Click it again to turn the filter off.
  • Sub-type, Set, Artist — narrow things down further.
  • Include / Exclude elements — show only cards of a certain element (Air, Earth, Fire, Water), or hide cards of one.
  • Rarity — filter by Ordinary, Exceptional, Elite, or Unique.
  • Stats & thresholds — filter by mana cost, attack, defense, or elemental threshold, using "equals," "at least," "at most," etc.

The number just below the filters tells you how many cards currently match. Click Reset filters at any time to clear everything and start over.

Building your deck

  • Click a card in the middle to add one copy to your deck.
  • Hold Shift and click a card to add four copies at once.
  • Click a card's picture or name to open its full details — right-click it if you want to see every bit of information the game data has for it.
  • In the deck list on the right, use the + / − buttons next to a card to adjust how many copies you have.
  • You can drag and drop cards within your deck list to reorder them.
  • Give your deck a name and pick a format at the top of the deck panel.
About copy limits: Sorcery normally limits how many copies of a card you can run based on its rarity (Ordinary: 4, Exceptional: 3, Elite: 2, Unique: 1). Grimoire will warn you if you go over, but it won't stop you — this is on purpose, since house rules and formats vary.

Your collection

The Collection box below your deck is a separate, personal list — useful for keeping track of extra cards you own that aren't part of the deck you're currently building (holds up to 10 cards). It's local to your computer only and isn't included when you export your deck.

Saving and opening decks

  • Grimoire automatically remembers your current deck as you work, even if you close the app — you won't lose it.
  • Save deck as… saves your deck to a file you choose, so you can back it up or share it.
  • Open… loads a deck file you saved earlier.
  • Clear deck empties the deck you're currently working on — use this when starting something new.

Sending your deck to Curiosa

Curiosa is a popular website for storing and sharing Sorcery decks. Grimoire can prepare a list you can paste straight into it:

  • Copy bulk-add list — copies your deck to the clipboard, ready to paste into Curiosa's "Show bulk add…" box.
  • Export for Curiosa (.txt) — saves the same list as a text file instead, in case you'd rather send the file to someone or paste it later.
Heads up: the list Grimoire creates does not include your Avatar or anything in your Collection — Curiosa asks you to pick your Avatar separately, and Collection cards aren't part of a deck. After pasting the list on Curiosa, just set your Avatar there manually.

Updating the card list

Grimoire comes with a full card list already built in, so you don't need to do anything to get started. If new cards have come out and you want the very latest list, click Update near the top of the window (you'll need an internet connection for this). If it can't connect, it will simply tell you and leave your existing card list untouched — nothing is lost.

The colored dot next to your card database status shows how things stand: green means everything loaded fine, yellow/red means there was an issue.

Card pictures

Card artwork is a separate download from the app itself (it's a large amount of data). If you're seeing plain text cards instead of pictures, ask whoever gave you this program for the card image folder, and place it where they tell you to. Cards without a picture on file will simply show as text-only tiles — everything else about them still works normally.

Something looks wrong

  • A card's stats look blank or off. Right-click the card to see everything Grimoire found about it — occasionally the official card data changes and a field may need to be looked at again.
  • No pictures are showing at all. See Card pictures above.
  • "Update" isn't working. This needs an internet connection — check that you're online. Your existing cards are safe either way.
  • Still stuck? Reach out to whoever shared Grimoire with you for help.
